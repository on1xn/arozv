    Uragirimono Project | SILENT HILL f Accessibility Mod By ON1XN

    You will probably understand the project name after playing the game.

    Uragirimono is an accessibility mod designed to make SILENT HILL f playable for blind and visually impaired players while preserving as much of the original gameplay, exploration, combat, atmosphere, and challenge as reasonably possible. This is a Public Test, not a claim that every part of the game has been fully verified from beginning to end.

    About SILENT HILL f

    SILENT HILL f is a third-person psychological horror game developed by NeoBards Entertainment and published by KONAMI. Set in 1960s Japan, it follows Shimizu Hinako through the isolated town of Ebisugaoka as she explores, solves puzzles, fights grotesque creatures, and experiences a story heavily influenced by Japanese culture, beliefs, imagery, and social context.

    The game combines exploration, puzzle solving, melee combat, psychological horror, and multiple endings. Some understanding of its Japanese setting and themes may help when interpreting the story.

    Download And Installation

    Download the mod here: Uragirimono Project

    Two packages are provided.

    1. Download the Uragirimono Bundled or Mod Only

    2. Open your SILENT HILL f installation directory.

    3. Find the folder containing SHf.exe.

    4. Extract or copy the contents of the ZIP directly into this folder.

    5. Start the game normally.

    6. Uragirimono should announce its name and version when loaded.

    What's Included

    Story & Exploration - Story and objective guidance across all 16 chapters. - Support for all five ending paths, including New Game+ requirements and optional quests. - Optional Chase Bypass, enabled by default.

    Menus & Accessibility - Spoken menus, inventory, equipment, documents, Notebook, save/load, and shrine interactions. - Dialogue, subtitles, and speech history. - Remote Interaction for supported objects. - Character and enemy descriptions. - Keyboard and controller support.

    Puzzles - Accessible puzzle interfaces with spoken instructions, clues, and interaction controls. - All puzzle difficulties are supported, including puzzles that change depending on your ending path. - Almost every puzzle can be solved independently. One puzzle is automatically solved because it could not be made independently accessible.

    Navigation & Scanner - Scanner categories for objectives, items, doors, enemies, NPCs, locations, and more. - Manual directional guidance, LB Lock, personal waypoints, and Teleport. - Auto Walk assistance where supported. - Directional audio for walls, open spaces, enemies, objectives, and incoming attacks.

    Customization - Adjustable sound cues, intervals, wall detection distance, and spoken guidance. - Optional obstacle, combat, HP, and Sanity announcements. - Customizable keyboard shortcuts. - 13 supported languages.

    Known Limitations

    Auto Walk does not cover every area or situation, and I currently have no plans for major improvements to it. Manual navigation is entirely possible with enough patience, and short-distance Teleport can help when needed.

    I strongly recommend keeping Chase Bypass enabled, especially for blind players. The chase sequences are extremely difficult to complete independently. But I won't stop you to try though.

    PS5 controller support has not been physically tested, as I don't own the hardware. If you'd like to help, I'm open to donations toward purchasing a controller or receiving one directly. It doesn't need to be brand new; a working second-hand controller is perfectly fine!

    Some situations may still require adjustments or additional testing.

    All achievement are not confirmed to be unlockable, Specially chase bypass. The rest are unlockable.

    Mod's Hotkeys

    Please read them in game by press LB + Back, L1 + D Pad up, or F1 on keyboard to open Uragirimono Menu, then Hotkeys. LB / RB, L1 / R1, Q and E change tab.
